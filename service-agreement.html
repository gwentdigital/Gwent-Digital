Service agreement

This page is the plain-English outline we use when preparing a project agreement. The signed agreement and written quote for your project always take priority.

Project details

The agreement names the client, project, deliverables, milestones, dependencies, price, payment dates and included revision rounds. Nothing is assumed to be included unless it is written down.

Deposit and payment

A 50% deposit reserves the project slot and covers initial planning and production. The balance is due when the agreed work is ready for handover. Monthly support plans are paid in advance.

Revisions and change requests

Included revisions are limited to the number stated in the quote. New pages, features, integrations or changes to an approved direction are treated as change requests and quoted before work continues.

Content, access and approvals

The client supplies accurate text, images, legal notices, account access and approvals. We may pause delivery when required information or feedback is late.

Handover and ownership

After final payment, we hand over the agreed final files, credentials and access needed to operate the service. We retain ownership of reusable internal tools, templates and know-how unless the quote says otherwise.

Hosting, domains and third-party services

Third-party subscriptions are normally held in the client's name where practical. Their fees, outages, policy changes and renewal terms are outside our control.

Support and defects

We correct faults in work delivered by us during the initial support period stated in the agreement. Ongoing updates, monitoring and content changes require a Care & Support plan or separate quote.

Cancellation and late payment

Either party may cancel before completion by written notice. Completed work, committed costs and work in progress remain payable. We may pause work and access to unpublished deliverables when invoices are overdue.

Acceptance

Approval may be recorded by email, message or signed confirmation. Once approved, later changes are handled under the revision and change-request terms above.

We will send a project-specific agreement for review before work starts. Questions can be sent to hello@gwentdigital.co.uk.
