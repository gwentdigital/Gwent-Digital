How it works

The jobs you do every week, done without you doing them

We run shops and a restaurant in Newport ourselves. So we start with the numbers, find where the money is actually leaking, and only then decide what to build.

Finding new customers

We build a list of local businesses that match the kind of customer you already have, draft a message for each one, and hold it for you to approve. Nothing sends on its own. Every contact is stored, and an opt-out is permanent.

Social media manager

Post ideas built from your own offers and your quiet days, captions written to sound like you, scheduled for the hours your customers are actually on their phones. You see every post before it goes out, and can edit or bin it.

The jobs nobody wants

Price labels printed straight from your stock list. Quotes and invoices raised from a form. Booking reminders. Review requests after a job. A weekly summary that tells you what happened without you opening five apps.

How it works
