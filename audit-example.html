Sample website audit

This is the type of short, practical review we send after looking at a local business website. It is an example format, not an audit of a real business.

High priority

Make the next step obvious

Put one clear action near the top of the page, such as “Call for a quote” or “Book online”. Repeat it after the main services so visitors do not have to hunt.

Suggested fix: use one primary button label consistently and make the phone number tappable on mobile.

Medium priority

Show proof beside the service

Visitors need to see finished work, customer feedback or a recognisable local signal before they commit.

Suggested fix: add three real project photos with a short description of the problem solved.

Quick win

Check local details

Your business name, phone number, opening hours and service area should match across the website and Google Business Profile.

Suggested fix: create one canonical contact block and use it everywhere.
