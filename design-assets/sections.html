My Punjab Desi Kitchen

Punjabi restaurant · Commercial Street, Newport · open about a year

The problem
A year in, with good food and quiet weekdays. Plenty of people within walking distance had simply never heard of it, and the online presence was not doing any selling.
What we did
Worked out who was actually within reach — students, people working in town, families — and wrote for each of them separately rather than posting the same thing at everyone. Built a 60-second ad around what the place actually is: food that tastes like home when you are a long way from it. Wrote the script, the story behind it and the captions to go with it, then put a turnaround plan together for the week-by-week running of it.

Fonetech Solutions

Phone and laptop sales and repair · Newport

The problem
Price labels were being written out by hand, so stock went on the shelf unpriced and customers had to ask. Separately, the shop was invisible online on exactly the days it most needed walk-ins.
What we did
Built a label system that prints straight to the shop’s thermal printer — one label per page, correct size for phones and for laptops, no fiddling with sheet layouts. Then set up a weekend offer routine: screen repair and battery deals, free diagnosis, and a case-and-glass bundle at a fixed price, posted to Newport local groups where people actually ask for recommendations.
