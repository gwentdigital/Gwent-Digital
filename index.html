Common headaches

Sound familiar?

These are the four problems we hear most from independent shop, trade and restaurant owners.

Your website takes forever to load — and customers leave before it finishes.

We fix this by stripping out bloat and optimising images and code for fast loading.

Your contact or booking form doesn’t actually work — enquiries vanish into thin air.

We fix this by testing every form end-to-end and making sure it reaches you properly.

You’re invisible on Google — customers search for you and find a competitor instead.

We fix this by setting up and linking your Google Business Profile with basic local SEO.

You’re still taking every booking over the phone — even mid-job, elbow-deep in work.

We fix this by adding online booking, WhatsApp auto-replies, or a form that works without you.

Privacy Policy

Plain English, no legal jargon. Here's exactly what happens to your details.

What we collect

When you use our contact form, we collect your name, business name, phone number, email address, what you need, and any message you write.

Why we collect it

Only to reply to your enquiry and provide you with a quote or audit. We do not add you to any marketing list or share your details with third parties for marketing.

How it's stored

Form submissions are handled securely by our form provider and delivered to our email inbox. We keep enquiry details only as long as needed to respond and, if you become a client, for our normal business records.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your details at any time by emailing hello@gwentdigital.co.uk. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) if you're unhappy with how we've handled your data.
